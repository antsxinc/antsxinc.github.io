The Open Core Model

The ANTsX GitHub organization hosts the world"s leading open-source ecosystem for quantitative imaging. It is the innovation engine—fluid, community-driven, and focused on academic novelty.

ANTsX Inc. is the commercial entity that bridges the gap between that academic innovation and industrial application. We operate on a model similar to Red Hat® relative to Linux®.

We do not sell the open-source code. We sell the certainty, stability, and scale required to use that code in high-stakes environments like Phase III clinical trials and FDA-regulated device development.

Technical Advantage: Generality & Meaning

Why choose the ANTsX ecosystem over other tools?

Most neuroimaging software is over-fitted to human T1 MRI. ANTsX is Universal.

We employ a generalized mathematical framework capable of processing any species, any modality, and any organ system.

Crucially, we do not just produce unlabeled maps. We align all data to known, established biological ontologies and further map these structures to hierarchies of function, performance, and cognition. This ensures your data is not only geometrically accurate but biologically interpretable.
